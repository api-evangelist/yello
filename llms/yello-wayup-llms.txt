# WayUp

WayUp is a hiring platform connecting college students and recent graduates with jobs and internships at top employers. Employers post entry-level positions, internships, and part-time roles; candidates build profiles, apply directly, and get discovered.

## Job & Internship Search

- [All Jobs & Internships](https://www.wayup.com/) — Search all open positions
- [Internships](https://www.wayup.com/s/internships/) — Paid and unpaid internships for current students
- [Entry-Level Jobs](https://www.wayup.com/s/entry-level-jobs/) — Full-time roles for recent graduates
- [Part-Time Jobs](https://www.wayup.com/s/part-time-jobs/) — Flexible part-time opportunities

## For Employers

- [Post a Job](https://www.wayup.com/employers/) — Reach qualified student and early-career candidates
- [Enterprise Hiring](https://www.wayup.com/employers/) — Diversity recruiting and campus hiring solutions
